# Clarifications (Phase 1)

## TL;DR
The "issue list" is the Lens Risks view. Follow-ups replaces it entirely. Extra stat: verdict breakdown. Follow-up sources: unfixed verification issues, open final risks, deferred decisions and e2e follow-ups.

## Q&A
- Issue list identity → Lens Risks view (the "Open risks" KPI view)
- Risks view fate → Replace with Follow-ups
- Extra stats → Verdict breakdown only
- Follow-up sources → Unfixed verification issues (dashboard-data.js `fixed != true`); Open final risks (unresolved phase_summaries risks); Deferred decisions and e2e_followup. NOT residual_issues/fixes_applied.
